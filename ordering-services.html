Ordering services

Flexible educational psychology support for schools, educational settings, parents and carers.

For schools and educational settings

Flexible packages of EP time

Educational settings can purchase packages of 2.5, 5, 10 or 20 days. Educational psychologist time is charged at a daily rate of £700, comprising 50% direct educational psychologist contact time and 50% back-up time.

£700 per day50% direct contact time · 50% preparation and follow-up
2.5 · 5 · 10 · 20 daysPackages can be combined to suit your setting

For example, a five-day package (30 hours) provides 15 hours of direct EP contact and 15 hours of back-up time for preparation, report writing and other follow-up work. Any combination of packages can be purchased, such as a ten-day package plus a 2.5-day package.

It may also be possible to purchase a day in-year, subject to EP availability and existing commitments. There is no additional charge for travel, photocopying or assessment materials.

When will reports be shared?

Reports will be shared with the school and parents or carers within four working weeks of the feedback meeting. During periods of high demand, the EP may need to agree revised timescales with you so expectations remain clear.

Service level agreements

Annual and additional support

Service level agreements run for one year and can be renewed annually. Additional traded EP time may be purchased during the year at the daily rate or through an additional package. Requests are considered individually and depend on EP availability.

Discrete requests from schools

Schools can place an order describing the support required. Spring Educational Psychology will acknowledge the request within five working days and provide a formal quotation.

Cancellation terms

Please contact us as early as possible if you need to cancel an appointment. There is no charge when at least two working days’ notice is provided.

Cancellation charges

With one to two working days’ notice: £250.
With less than one working day’s notice: the full price, as the reserved appointment cannot usually be offered to somebody else.
