Supporting Children to Learn, Grow and Thrive

At Spring Educational Psychology, we work alongside schools, families and young people to create positive, lasting change.

We provide high-quality, flexible educational psychology services, including consultation, assessment, intervention, supervision and training. Our approach is collaborative and tailored to each child, school and situation.

We support a wide range of learning, emotional, behavioural, communication, sensory and additional needs, always considering the people and environment surrounding each child.

Our aim is simple: to help children and young people feel understood, overcome barriers and reach their potential.

All our Educational Psychologists are HCPC registered.

Still unsure whether educational psychology is right for you?

Ask Rebecca ↗
Do you diagnose ADHD, autism or dyslexia?+

Spring does not typically provide formal diagnosis. The focus is on understanding strengths and needs, exploring possible explanations and identifying useful support.

What happens during an initial consultation?+

We discuss strengths, concerns and what you hope involvement will achieve, then agree the scope, costs and appropriate next steps.

How much does an assessment cost?+

Costs depend on the questions and level of involvement needed. You’ll receive a clear, proportionate quotation before any work is agreed.

Do you work face-to-face?+

Yes. Face-to-face work is available across Buckinghamshire, Berkshire and Oxfordshire. Online services are available where appropriate.

What if an EP isn’t what we need?+

The initial conversation is professional advice, not a sales process. If educational psychology is not the right support, Rebecca will tell you.

Meet Rebecca

Experience, expertise
& qualifications

Rebecca is a skilled Chartered Educational and Child Psychologist registered with the Health & Care Professions Council (HCPC). She has over twenty years of experience working with children and vulnerable young people within educational settings and beyond. Rebecca is also a member of the Association of Educational Psychologists and British Psychological Society.

Rebecca has worked as an Educational Psychologist and Senior Specialist Educational Psychologist (wellbeing) in Buckinghamshire and the Royal Borough of Windsor and Maidenhead (RBWM) Local Authorities. Alongside continuing her Senior Specialist Educational Psychologist role in RBWM, Rebecca also managed the Wellbeing and Systemic Wellbeing teams, working in collaboration with both Early Help and Social Care Services.

Before training as an Educational Psychologist, Rebecca was a special educational needs Teaching Assistant, a primary school Teacher and Special Educational Needs Co-ordinator at schools in Hampshire and London.

Rebecca is particularly adept at utilising systems theory and evidence-based practice to promote wellbeing, as well as promoting attachment-aware and nurture-based approaches. Rebecca is an advocate of person-centred planning processes for young people and organisations, and for the use of consultation and joint problem-solving approaches for professionals.

Rebecca’s main areas of interest are emotional literacy and emotion coaching, eliciting children’s views, executive functioning, autism and social communication differences, emotionally based school avoidance, consultation and coaching, Personal Construct Psychology, and solution-focused and narrative approaches. In recent years, supporting the understanding of children with complex learning, social and emotional difficulties and mental health needs has featured strongly in her work.
